Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2025

Rice Plus Grill ("we," "our," "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you place an order with us via phone call, text message, or our website at riceplusgrill.com.

Information We Collect

  • Phone number — used to send order confirmations and payment links via SMS
  • Name — used to identify your order and personalize service
  • Delivery address — used only for delivery orders
  • Order details — items ordered, total amount, and order preferences
  • Payment information — processed securely through Stripe; we do not store your card number

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and fulfill your food orders
  • To send SMS messages with order confirmations, payment links, and status updates
  • To provide customer service and respond to inquiries
  • To improve our menu offerings and service quality

SMS Messaging

By placing an order, you consent to receive SMS messages from us. Message frequency is typically 1 to 3 messages per order. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. Standard message and data rates may apply from your wireless carrier.

Mobile Information & Messaging Consent — Not Shared

Mobile information and messaging consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number or SMS messaging consent with any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Your phone number is used solely for order-related communications (order confirmations, payment links, and order status updates) as described in this Privacy Policy.

Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Your information is shared only with:

  • Stripe — for secure payment processing (PCI DSS compliant)
  • Twilio — for sending SMS messages to your phone number for order-related purposes only
  • Service providers — as needed to fulfill your order (e.g., delivery drivers)

Neither Stripe nor Twilio receives your mobile information or messaging consent for marketing or promotional purposes. They receive only the minimum data needed to process your payment and deliver your SMS message.

Data Retention

We retain your order history and contact information for as long as needed to provide service and comply with legal requirements. You may request deletion of your information at any time by calling us at (856) 856-2202.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information. Payment data is handled exclusively by Stripe, which is PCI DSS compliant. We do not store credit card numbers, CVV codes, or other sensitive payment details.

Your Rights

  • Request access to your personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Opt out of SMS messaging at any time (reply STOP)

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
