SMS Consent & Terms

By placing an order with Rice Plus Grill via phone call, text message, or our website, you consent to receive SMS text messages from us at the phone number you provide. These messages may include:

  • Order confirmations — confirmation of your order details and total
  • Payment links — secure links to complete your payment online
  • Order status updates — notifications when your order is being prepared or is ready for pickup/delivery
  • Customer service responses — replies to your questions or inquiries

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on your orders. Typically, you will receive 1 to 3 messages per order. You will not receive marketing or promotional messages unless you explicitly opt in.

How to Opt In

You opt in to SMS messaging by:

  • Calling our restaurant at (856) 856-2202 and placing an order
  • Texting your order to (856) 856-2202
  • Placing an order on our website at riceplusgrill.com with your phone number

How to Opt Out

You can opt out of SMS messaging at any time by:

  • Replying STOP to any message you receive from us
  • Replying UNSUBSCRIBE to any message
  • Calling us at (856) 856-2202 and requesting to be removed from our text list

After opting out, you will receive no further SMS messages from us. You may re-opt in at any time by placing a new order.

Help & Support

Reply HELP to any message for assistance, or call us directly at (856) 856-2202. Message and data rates may apply from your wireless carrier.

Mobile Information & Messaging Consent — Not Shared

Mobile information and messaging consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number or SMS messaging consent with any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Your phone number is used solely for order-related communications as described on this page.

Privacy

Your phone number and order information are used solely for order processing and customer service. We do not sell or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes. For full details, see our Privacy Policy.
